# Dungeon Evolution — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

Dungeon Evolution is a single-player game. It does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

- **No accounts, no sign-in.** The game never asks who you are.
- **No analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The game contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- **No network use.** Everything the game needs is inside the app; it doesn't send anything over the internet.
- **Saves stay on your device.** Your progress, volume and reduce-motion setting are kept in the app's local storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
- **No permissions beyond the basics.** The game doesn't access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Google Play may collect data about installs and crashes for the developer as part of its own services; that is covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
